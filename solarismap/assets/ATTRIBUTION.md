# Asset attribution

The art in this directory is vendored from the
[Solaris custom galaxy editor](https://github.com/IHateAttackMaps/solaris-custom-galaxy-editor)
(`src/assets/`), which is itself a derivative of
[Solaris](https://github.com/solaris-games/solaris). It is kept here so a render looks like
the game rather than like a diagram of the game.

This repository is GPL-3.0, like the editor it derives from. See [../../LICENSE](../../LICENSE).

## specialists/

Icons made by the [game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net) contributors, provided under
**Creative Commons 3.0 BY**, or CC0 where the original license file says so. The full
contributor list ships alongside them in [specialists/license.txt](specialists/license.txt),
which is the upstream file unmodified — game-icons.net asks that derivative works carry a
mention of the form *"Icons made by {author}"*, and that list is the authoritative set of
authors for these 67 icons.

Named contributors include Lorc, Delapouite, John Colburn, Felbrigg, John Redman, Carl Olsen,
Sbed, PriorBlue, Willdabeast, Viscious Speed, Lord Berandas, Irongamer, HeavenlyDog,
Lucas, Faithtoken, Skoll, Cathelineau, Kier Heyl, Aussiesim, Sparker, Zeromancer, Rihlsul,
Quoting, Guard13007, DarkZaitzev, SpencerDub, GeneralAce135, Zajkonur, Catsu, Starseeker,
Pepijn Poolman and Pierre Leducq.

## map-objects/, stars/, nebula/

Star glyphs, player shape rings, warp-gate rings, carrier glyphs, and the nebula, asteroid
field and wormhole vortex textures, from the editor's own asset set.

## What was left behind

`src/assets/nebula/neb0-*.png` and `neb1-*.png` are canvas backdrop art for the editor's
WebGL view, roughly 4 MB, and nothing here draws them. `src/assets/selection/` (MIT,
Copyright (c) 2021 Kamila Graf) was cursor art for the editor's selection tool and is
likewise unused by the renderer.
