# Spy v. Spy — Irregular

**36 players · 9 galaxies · 36 wormholes**

*The same skeleton, grown rather than placed.*

This is [Spy v. Spy](#/spy-v-spy) with every galaxy grown by a generator instead of laid out by
hand. Nine pods on a ring, four players to a pod, one wormhole for every pair of galaxies — but
no two galaxies are the same shape, so fairness here is measured rather than inherited from
symmetry.
